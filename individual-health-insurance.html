Frequently Asked Questions

What health insurance options are available for healthy individuals? +
🌻 Healthy individuals may have access to several coverage options depending on their state, eligibility, and healthcare needs. Plans can vary in monthly premiums, deductibles, provider networks, and benefits. Bright Bloom Health helps clients compare available options to find coverage that fits both their budget and lifestyle.
Can I keep my doctor? +
🌻 Many plans include broad provider networks, including PPO options that allow greater flexibility when choosing healthcare providers. During your consultation, we'll help verify whether your preferred doctors and specialists participate in available networks.
Do PPO plans require referrals? +
🌻 A PPO (Preferred Provider Organization) plan allows members to visit specialists without referrals and often provides access to a larger network of providers. Many clients prefer PPO plans because of their flexibility and nationwide accessibility.
What if I'm self-employed? +
🌻 Yes. Self-employed individuals have several health insurance options available depending on their circumstances. We help business owners compare plans and determine which solution best fits their needs and budget.
Can I get coverage outside of Open Enrollment? +
🌻 Open Enrollment in the traditional sense is for ACA Marketplace plans or employer options. Private plans can be enrolled in during any time of the year.
