What Is an ICHRA?

An Individual Coverage Health Reimbursement Arrangement (ICHRA) allows employers to reimburse employees for qualified health insurance expenses rather than sponsoring a traditional group health plan.

Many businesses appreciate ICHRAs because they can:

  • ✓ Control benefit budgets
  • ✓ Offer flexibility to employees
  • ✓ Support remote and multi-state teams
  • ✓ Simplify benefit administration
  • ✓ Potentially reduce overall healthcare costs

Bright Bloom Health helps businesses understand whether an ICHRA strategy aligns with their goals and assists employees throughout the enrollment process.
